# Baseline governance

- Product requirements are owned by `specs/002-ss-casolab-academy/spec.md`.
- Architecture and operating decisions are owned by `specs/002-ss-casolab-academy/plan.md` and its contracts.
- Executable work and completion states are owned by `specs/002-ss-casolab-academy/tasks.md`.
- Legal and examination facts require a direct official source and consultation date.
- Draft educational content is not publishable until it passes the normative and editorial checklists.
- Moodle is a delivery target, not the canonical editorial source.
- No capture, order or payment path may be presented as active before its legal and operational prerequisites are real.
